Understanding R-multiples
R = Risk unit. Before entering a trade you decide: "I'm willing to lose X dollars on this." That amount is your 1R. Every outcome below is expressed as a multiple of that number — so you can compare trades regardless of position size.
+2R You made 2× your risk Risked $100 → walked away with +$200 profit
~0R Break even Exited near entry — neither gained nor lost
−1R Stop hit — full loss Risked $100 → stop triggered, lost $100
Your formula: pick any risk amount (e.g. $500) → multiply by the R shown to get your dollar outcome. +1.07R on $500 = +$535 profit. −1R on $500 = −$500 loss. The "per $100 risked" figures in the cards below use this same logic.
