Active trades

Trades you marked as "Took it". Original SL/TP are from when you took the trade (never overwritten). Suggested SL/TP are from the latest pipeline run so you can compare.

Original vs suggested. Your original stop-loss and take-profit are fixed. Every ~2h the app computes suggested levels from the latest price; they appear in the table and in the per-trade history so you can see all runs and timestamps.
