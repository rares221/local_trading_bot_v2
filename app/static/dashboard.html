Key Concepts

Market Regime

Classification of market conditions based on SPX trend and VIX level:

  • Risk-On: Bullish trend + low volatility → more aggressive recommendations, higher confidence thresholds relaxed
  • Cautious: Mixed signals or moderate volatility → balanced approach, standard thresholds
  • Risk-Off: Bearish trend or high volatility → defensive stance, may flip longs to shorts, stricter confidence filters

Confidence Score & Buckets

Each recommendation gets a 0-100 confidence score based on sentiment + technical alignment. Scores are grouped into buckets:

  • Low (0-40): Weak signals, lower probability
  • Medium (40-60): Moderate conviction
  • High (60-80): Strong alignment
  • Very High (80-100): Exceptional setup

Calibration uses historical outcomes to ensure scores reflect actual win rates.

Position Sizing & Risk Budget

Suggested Risk %: Calculated using half-Kelly formula from confidence and risk-reward ratio. Higher confidence + better R:R = larger suggested position.

Risk Budget: Caps the sum of every trade’s Size % in the batch. Default 25 fits up to ~20 recommendations (5 per category); raise it if the engine logs risk-budget skips.
