Understanding Outcomes & Metrics

Outcomes:

  • Hit TP: Price reached take-profit target (win)
  • Hit SL: Price hit stop-loss (loss)
  • Expired: Trade reached expiry date without hitting TP/SL
  • Manual: User manually closed the trade

Win Rate: Hit TP / (Hit TP + Hit SL). Excludes expired and manual closes. Target: 55%+ for profitable system with good risk-reward ratios.

Confidence Buckets: Use filters to see if higher confidence trades actually perform better. Well-calibrated system should show increasing win rates for higher buckets.
