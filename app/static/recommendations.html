Understanding Recommendations (click to expand)

Confidence Score: 0-100 rating based on sentiment + technical alignment. Higher = stronger conviction. Grouped into buckets: Low (0-40), Medium (40-60), High (60-80), Very High (80-100).

Calibrated (cal): When Platt scaling is fitted on the Dashboard (≥10 closed trades), the Conf column shows a second line: model-adjusted score on the same 0-100 scale. Compare raw vs cal to see optimism or pessimism vs historical outcomes.

B (shadow): If A/B shadow mode is on in the Dashboard when the batch is built, B shows whether that row would pass a second confidence gate (live regime min + your delta). Live picks are unchanged; this is for comparison only.

Size: Suggested position size (% of capital at risk) calculated from confidence and risk-reward using half-Kelly formula.

Market Regime: Current conditions affect which assets appear and their direction. Risk-On = more aggressive longs; Risk-Off = defensive, may flip to shorts.
