💡 Understanding Recommendations (click to expand)

Confidence Score: 0–100 from sentiment + technical alignment. Buckets: Low (0–40), Medium (40–60), High (60–80), Very High (80–100). On the Dashboard you can fit Platt calibration on closed trades so scores map more closely to realized hit rates.

Size: Suggested % of capital at risk (half-Kelly style from confidence and R:R). The risk budget on the Dashboard caps the sum of Size % across all ideas in one batch.

Market Regime: SPX trend + VIX drive risk-on, cautious, or risk-off. Regime changes which names qualify, direction (e.g. equities may flip short in risk-off), and confidence thresholds.

Context column (one long line of tags — the table is wide so it wraps):

  • Leading phrase — Short market summary when the row was built; may add sentiment–price divergence (news vs price disagree) or high-impact event (Fed, macro, earnings tags from the news pipeline).
  • ATR / RSI / MACD / ADX / Stoch / OBV / Bollinger / S&R — Snapshot of indicators and nearest support (S) / resistance (R) used for direction and SL/TP.
  • MTF — Multi-timeframe trend: aligned vs conflicting with the trade direction (daily / weekly / 4H when available).
  • EMA — EMA stack label (bullish / bearish / mixed) from the same alignment module.
  • Vol regime — contracting ATR → slightly wider stop/target bands to reduce whipsaw; expanding → slightly tighter bands; stable → baseline ATR multiples.
  • Macro calendar — On heavy macro days (e.g. FOMC, CPI, NFP window) the engine applies a small confidence penalty across candidates; that isn’t repeated in every cell but affects the whole batch.

Indicator conflict: Ideas above the skip threshold are removed. Survivors may show indicator conflict penalty: N (skip if ≥25) in Context so you can see how “tight” the fit was.
